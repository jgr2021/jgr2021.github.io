**Authors:** Gurui Jin, Pengcheng Hao, Zhenyu Liu, Xiao-Ping Zhang, Ercan E. Kuruoglu

This work studies robust state estimation when measurement noise contains both a Gaussian component and a heavy-tailed Student's t component. The proposed RKF-AGST explicitly models this additive hybrid-noise structure within a Kalman-filtering framework.

In the target-tracking simulations summarized in my current CV, the method achieved a **12.27% RMSE reduction** relative to the standard Kalman filter.
